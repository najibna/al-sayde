Alsayde Festival

St. Mary church is pleased to announce the return of its annual festival which will take place on August 14, 15 and 16, 2026.

Thanks to the strategic support of our partners, our festival has become a must-attend event, bringing together thousands of participants each year. In addition to our strong local roots, our digital presence is massive: our social media posts generate over 450,000 views.

We now offer you the opportunity to associate your company’s image with this dynamic and engaging event. It’s the perfect opportunity to boost your visibility with an audience of all ages, while demonstrating your community involvement.

To meet your business and marketing objectives, we have structured our offerings into several levels of impact. The various packages are described on the following page.

We would be delighted to discuss the package that best suits your growth strategy.

Church Office: 514-858-7004 (Professor Seraphim Daoud or Professor Elias Ferzli)

You can also express your interest by email:
festivalsayde@gmail.com
Please include:

  • The name and phone number of the contact person;
  • The full company name as desired on the invoice;
  • The logo or advertisement to be displayed in PNG or JPG format;
  • The package you prefer.

Please note that the deadline to receive your poster advertisement is August 3, 2026.

We thank you in advance for your generosity.

Cordially,

Organizing Committee of the 2025 Festival

Pascale Daher, Nicolas Badran, Elie Hallak, Lena Abou Haidar, Pierette Bhamdouni, Spiro Demian, Fares Abou Haidar

Logo printed on the category’s group poster.

Gold Partner $1500:
