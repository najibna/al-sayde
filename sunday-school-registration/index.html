Sunday School Registration

Payment for Sunday School Registration for 2026-2027

The Registration fees for this year will be as follows: $40 for one child, $60 for two children, and $75 for three children. These token fees will be used to fuel our recreational activities (Christmas party, Easter activity, and other activities throughout the year) and to cover the cost of used supplies (papers, printing, pens, etc.).

Please check your email (including Spam/Junk) for the payment confirmation.

Donation Refund Policy

If an error occurred with respect to your donation, or if you have decided to withdraw your contribution to St. Mary’s Antiochian Orthodox Church in Montreal, we will accept and process your request for a refund within a period of 15 days of your donation. Refunds are returned using the original payment method. Donations made by credit card will be credited to that same credit card. To request a refund, please contact the church at (514) 858-7004.

Contact information

St. Mary’s Antiochian Orthodox Church in Montreal, 10841 Grande Allée, Montreal, QC, H3L 2M8, Tel: 1(514) 858-7004, Email: alsayde@alsayde.org

Services

Matins every Sunday at 10:00 AM. Divine Liturgy every Sunday at 11:00 AM.
