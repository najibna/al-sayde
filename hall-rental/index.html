Hall Rental

Saint Mary Antiochian Orthodox Church offers two rooms for rent for your professional and recreational events.

The large room measures 28 x 15.5 m (90 x 50 ft)

The small room measures 14×7 m (45×25 ft)

Rental prices vary depending on the duration and type of event. We will contact you as soon as possible after receiving your request.
